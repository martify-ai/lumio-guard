Checking protection…

    Private (Incognito) windows are not protected. To fix this, open Chrome's extension settings for Lumio Guard and turn on "Allow in Incognito".

    What Lumio Guard can't do yet

    • It only works in this Chrome browser, not other browsers or phone apps.
    • It blocks known sites on its lists. New or unknown sites can get through.
    • Safe Search covers Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo and YouTube only.
    • It counts time in this browser only, not total screen time.
    • It can be turned off from Chrome's extension settings.

    Everything stays on this computer. Lumio Guard sends nothing anywhere.
